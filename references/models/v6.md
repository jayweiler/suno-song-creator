| Model | Plan | Suno's description | Use for |

| v6 | Pro / Premier | "reliable, precise and consistently delivers polished music across every genre and style" | finished tracks, brief-following |
| v6-wild | Pro / Premier | "less predictable and more varied, producing unexpected, textured and ambitious results" | exploration, genre blends, genres v6 flattens |

## Headline differences from v5.5

- **New corpus, new defaults.** Same prompt on Duration=Auto comes back "slower, sparser,
  often longer" (community, GitHub issue 562). A day-one A/B rated v6 above v5.5 on vocal
  flow, delivery and instrumentation (hookgenius). Top Reddit complaint: "musically
  lifeless and sounds the most synthetic" (u/Songgeek, 51 upvotes, via Undetectr 9/10).
  Reports split by genre: pop, rock, folk, techno positive; metal, heavy guitar, DnB negative.
- **Variety slider (new).** Off / Normal / High / Extra / Max. Suno: "designed to introduce
  variety in your outputs by adjusting and updating your style prompts." Above Off, Suno
  REWRITES your style text before generating. Suno's FAQ: to keep full control of your
  style tags, reduce Variety to 0 (verified 11/21). Default Normal on v6 and v6-mini, Off on
  v6-wild (GitHub 562, single source).
- **Max Mode (new toggle).** More compute, more credits (2x per community reports; Suno
  states no multiple). Suno recommends it for songs over
  ~2 minutes, covers, Voices, style transfer, and vocal consistency across a track.
  Community: standard mode thins out after ~2:30 (unverified).
- **Simple mode is agentic and multimodal.** One plain-language request can edit a section,
  swap a lyric, mash up your own songs, sample/isolate a beat, or take up to 5 images, 1
  video (<= 241 s) or 5 audio refs. Caveat: Simple mode expands supplied lyrics instead of
  singing them verbatim (GitHub 562). Finished-lyrics work stays in Advanced (Custom) mode.
  Section edit and single-lyric edit are official: "change one word or line while leaving
  the rest intact."
- **Polish is the default.** Cleaner vocals, wider mixes, less ad-lib doubling, fewer
  unrequested elements. Suno: "if you want imperfect vocals, write that directly into your prompt."

## Prompt format

**Narrative prose**, as with v5/v5.5; Suno's own v6 examples moved from tag lists to "a
more conversational prompt" (hookgenius, citing Suno docs). Suno: v6 "understands more of
the language and building blocks musicians use, from vocals, instrumentation and structure
to mood, references and the overall feel of a song."

- Layer it: genre + groove/tempo + defining instruments + vocal character + emotional
  movement + production texture + arrangement behavior. Front-load; the opening phrase
  still carries the most weight.
- Positive statements only in Style. Rejections go in **Exclude Styles**, never in the
  prose. Naming an unwanted element in Style tends to summon it.
- Ask for imperfection explicitly (cracked vocal, tape hiss, loose timing); v6 adds no grit unasked.
- Numbers beat adjectives: "120 BPM" over "medium tempo"; "4 bars" over "short intro."
- Editing: one transformation per request; name sections structurally (chorus, bridge)
  rather than by timestamp.
- Artist names: still blocked or rewritten, and "inspired by X" counts. Describe the sonic
  fingerprint. Partner-artist opt-in products are "phase two," not in v6 at launch.
- Settings that travel with the prompt: **Variety = Off** whenever the Style box is
  engineered; Style Influence (default 50%, Loose to Strong) at 75 to 85 for strict
  adherence (community); Weirdness default 50%. Typing "Weirdness 20%" into Style does nothing.

## Style char limit & truncation

- Style: **1000 chars.** Lyrics: **5000.** Exclude Styles 1000 and Simple-mode prompt 3000.
  All four match the official connector's parameter limits (verified 11/21). With Variety Off the stored style text is a byte-for-byte
  prefix of what you pasted (hookgenius test).
- 400 to 800 well-formed chars beat a full box. Front-load so truncation eats the tail.
- Lyrics: 30 to 40 lines is the sweet spot; over ~60 gets rushed (creator guide, unverified on v6).
- Duration lives in Advanced Options (Auto or a target). Behavior when lyrics do not fit a
  custom Duration (rushed, dropped sections, hard cut) is unverified.
Sources: https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-v6-guide/ https://github.com/bitwize-music-studio/claude-ai-music-skills/issues/562 https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-character-limits/ https://note.com/aimusicworks/n/ne911e3ec54aa

## Cue reliability

- Section tags and parenthetical cues carry over unchanged; Suno has published no v6 tag
  spec ("hints, not commands"). Day-one test: v6 actively reads **per-section** delivery.
  A whispered French bridge specified only in lyric cues showed up in the output, so
  describe delivery per section, not just for the song (hookgenius).
- **Dual instruction** works best: say the section behavior in Style prose AND restate it
  in the bracket, e.g. `[Intro - guitar riff, 4 bars, instrumental only]`. Bar counts and
  instrument names outperform bare `[Intro]` / `[Outro]` (Undetectr).
- Variety above Off can override cues and exclusions, because it rewrites the style text.
- Take-to-take variance can exceed a deliberate prompt change; judge a cue on 2+ takes.
- Brackets sit on their own line. Singable-looking prose in the Lyrics field may get sung.
- Unverified on v6: bar-count tags like `[VERSE 1 8]`; Exclude Styles vs group vocals; v6-mini cue obedience.
Sources: https://hookgenius.app/learn/suno-v6-guide/ https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-intro-outro-prompts https://github.com/bitwize-music-studio/claude-ai-music-skills/issues/562 https://jackrighteous.com/en-us/pages/suno-ai-meta-tags-guide

## v6-specific UI & features

- Model dropdown, top right of Create: v6 / v6-wild / v6-mini.
- Create modes: **Simple** (agentic, multimodal), **Advanced** (the old Custom: Lyrics,
  Styles, audio reference + influence, Cover), **Sounds** (one-shots, loops, BPM/key).
- Advanced Options: Exclude Styles, Vocal Gender, Duration, Max Mode, Weirdness, Style
  Influence, Audio Influence, Variety, My Taste (Personalize). Disabling Personalize does
  not stop Variety rewrites.
- Remix/Edit: Extend, Cover, Replace Section, Remaster, all "powered by v6," plus section
  edit, single-lyric edit, mashups (own catalog only), sample and isolate. Use as
  Inspiration / Inspo persists as audio references (up to 5 in Simple mode).
- **Voices replaced the Personas button**; "Style Personas are still available within the
  Voices menu" (Suno Voices FAQ). One-click "Upgrade Voice to v6" reported; Voices cannot
  be used on instrumental tracks (GitHub 562). **Custom Models auto-upgraded to v6** (Suno
  FAQ); Pro/Premier, up to 3, 6+ owned songs (24+ and 100 credits reported, single source).
- Stems: the official connector prices Get Stems at 10 credits for vocals plus instrumental
  (two takes at 5) and 50 credits for the 12-part split (two takes at 25) (verified 11/21;
  this replaces an earlier community figure). Song to MIDI: 10 credits, Studio plans.
  Studio 2.0 (Aug 13,
  Premier) exports up to 12 stems at 32-bit/48 kHz without using download quota.
- Downloads (from Sept 3): Free 7 lifetime, Pro 20/month, Premier 60/month, $2.99 each
  beyond; commercial rights attach to paid downloads. ToS forbids stripping the fingerprint.

## Settings through a connector

When the skill generates through Suno's MCP connector, the UI settings above map to
parameters. The connector schema is the authority; see `../connector.md`.

| On suno.com | Connector parameter | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Model dropdown | `model` | Labels `v6`, `v6-wild`, `v6-mini`. Send only labels in the account's `allowed_models` |
| Styles | `style` | 1000 chars |
| Exclude Styles | `negative_style` | 1000 chars. Needs advanced controls on the plan |
| Variety Off / Normal / High / Extra / Max | `controls.variety` 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 | 0 keeps the style tags as written |
| Style Influence 0 to 100% | `controls.style_influence` 0 to 1 | 75% is 0.75 |
| Weirdness 0 to 100% | `controls.weirdness` 0 to 1 | Omit to keep the model default |
| Audio Influence | `controls.audio_influence` 0 to 1 | On a new song, only with a voice. On covers, extensions and mashups it is closeness to the source |
| Vocal Gender | `vocal_gender` | `male` or `female` |
| Duration | `duration` 15 to 360 s | A hard cap by default when set. Omit it for a full song with finished lyrics |
| Max Mode | `max_mode` | Only when the user asks for Max Mode by name |
| MIDI score input | `midi_upload_id` | Simple mode only; the schema says it needs `v6-wild` |

Finished lyrics go in custom mode (`lyrics` plus `style`). Simple mode (`description`) writes
or expands lyrics itself, the same caveat as on the site.

## Pitfalls (community reports, 9/9 to 9/17)

- Over-polish: "synthetic" instruments, distorted guitars "absolutely awful," long songs
  thinning to "barely just chords and drums" in the back half on standard mode.
- Variety silently rewriting prompt libraries ("shorter and weaker" until Off); Simple mode
  expanding pasted lyrics.
- v6-wild: roughly half of takes "broken and unusable" for one tester. Sketch, then re-cover in v6.
- No rollback. Old-model Personas whose vocal character is gone will not come back.
- Contradictions: Voices FAQ still says v5.5 (stale vs upgrade reports); Personas "gone"
  (thevocalmarket) vs "still in the Voices menu" (Suno FAQ wins). YouTube A/Bs not reviewable.
Sources: https://undetectr.com/blog/suno-v6-reactions https://roo.beehiiv.com/p/suno-v6-features https://thevocalmarket.com/blogs/how-to/suno-v6-what-it-means-for-producers https://makno.substack.com/p/suno-v6-manuel-dutilisation

## When to choose which

- **v6:** any finished song, especially with Voices or a Custom Model. Suno recommends Max
  Mode past ~2 minutes or when a vocal must stay consistent. It costs more, so it is the
  user's call: mention it, never switch it on for them.
- **v6-wild:** the song wants surprise, a genre blend, or lives in a genre v6 flattens
  (metal, heavy guitar, DnB). Expect to discard half the takes.
- **v6-mini:** lyric and structure checks before spending Pro credits. Older models are
  not selectable; for the raw / lo-fi aesthetics v5 handled, write the grit in and try wild.

## Example prompt

Settings: Variety Off, Style Influence 75. Exclude: "strings, synth pads, gang vocals, fade out".
"Melancholic indie-folk, sung by an intimate, slightly worn male voice recorded close to
the mic with audible breath and room tone. Fingerpicked nylon guitar carries the verses
alone. A warm upright bass enters in verse two and holds long tones from there. Brushed
drums arrive only at the final chorus, kept quiet. Around 92 BPM in D major. Dry mix,
plenty of negative space, no reverb wash, no triumphant lift at the end."
